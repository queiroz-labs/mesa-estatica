# Ícone do Estática

Arte aprovada pelo usuário em 24/10/2026: d20 fraturado por estática com olho âmbar, contornos ciano e fundo grafite azulado. Criada com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens; o monograma corporativo anterior foi descartado.

- `estatica-icon-source.png`: original aprovado.
- `../../public/estatica-icon.png`: versão de 512 × 512 para reutilização.
- `../../public/favicon-32.png`: favicon PNG de 32 × 32.
- `../../public/favicon.ico`: favicon com tamanhos 16, 32, 48 e 64 px.

Derivados apenas redimensionados e convertidos, sem alteração da composição. As duas entradas HTML usam os mesmos favicons. Os arquivos finais são locais e dispensam geração ou ferramentas gráficas durante o build.

## Prompt da arte aprovada

```text
Use case: logo-brand / stylized-concept.
Asset type: ONE finished square game icon / favicon for "Estática", an investigative HORROR tabletop RPG in dystopian São Paulo.
IMPORTANT creative direction: This must immediately feel like an eerie tabletop ROLEPLAYING GAME artifact, never a corporate logo or software company. No letters, no monograms, no wordmark, no business emblem.
Subject: a large haunted twenty-sided die (D20), angular icosahedral silhouette viewed from a corner, subtly cracked open across its middle by one jagged horizontal static-signal fracture. A single ominous almond-shaped eye with a tall black slit pupil peers out from the center of the die. The eye should be the memorable focal point, uncanny and watchful, not cute, not an Egyptian symbol. A few bold triangular facet lines make the die recognizable, not a dense wireframe. The fracture is offset like analog TV interference. The die feels like a cursed physical tabletop artifact, with broad hand-inked engraving and slightly chipped corners rather than a sterile tech symbol.
Composition: centered icon fills 85% of square, generous strong silhouette that survives 16 or 32 pixels. Flat frontal emblem illustration with subtle dimensional facet shading, minimal large shapes. No tiny decorations. Background full-bleed uniform blue charcoal #0b0d11. Die planes blue charcoal #1c2028; thick edges and fracture in muted clinical cyan #4fc1d4; central eye pale amber #c99a5a with a dark pupil. No red, no green neon, no pure white. Eye and cyan die readable against background.
Mood: analog horror, paranormal investigation, forbidden dice, mysterious tabletop RPG. Graphic inked game inventory icon, restrained dark pulp illustration, gritty through silhouette and fractured shapes, not photographic grain.
Do not include text, numbers, letters, titles, mockups, presentation layout, grids, cards, border frame, gradients in background, flashy glow, fine texture, tiny scratch noise, weapons, shields, coats of arms, medieval ornament, extra eyes, skulls. Only the die, central eye, and static fracture on dark background. Deliver the actual square production icon.
```
